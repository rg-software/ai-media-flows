The clip becomes ONE `IMAGE` batch that several nodes consume (SAM3 tracking, RT-DETR, the body model, MoGe, the render), and a 720x1280 float32 frame is ~11 MiB, so there is a real per-frame cost - but **the peak is dominated by model residency, not the batch**, and it is therefore NOT a clean function of frame count: 290 frames peaked *lower* (21 629 MiB) than 242 frames did (22 142 MiB), depending on the phase and what was cached. **The ceiling, measured rather than extrapolated: 290 frames (12 s) completes, 363 frames (15 s) dies with a hard CUDA OOM at 31 MiB free** - so ~12 s at 720x1280, not the ~15 s a linear fit suggested (that fit was optimistic by ~25%, and the OOM is what corrected it).
