# Audio Experiments Log

One row per run. Verdicts: `PASS` / `FAIL` / `MEH` / `BLOCKED`.

This is the third scenario born from a modality with the node surface already installed and the weights missing - `video/` was the first, and the method is the same: scope by what the nodes need, read the licence, fetch the weights, then run one flow and **measure** it. `PIPELINES.md` was written for this: audio arrives as new `domain` values on the existing operation families (`generate / scene / audio`), not as a new family, and its `subject` vocabulary is the open question recorded there (`clip`, `cue`).

**We have no ears.** Every verdict here rests on measurable proxies plus a spectrogram - see the traps in `README.md`. That is a real limitation of this scenario, stated up front rather than discovered later.

The mechanism is that blending the segment's tail into its head *sums two uncorrelated passages*, and the percussion in each lines up. So for an ambient bed the recipe is the inverse of the usual advice: **cut from the middle and join hard**. **Levels stay per-take:** the stinger peaks **0.0 dBFS** (a transient in a 5 s burst) while the bed is comfortable at **-1.6** - E2/E3/E4 have all now found this, so `peak` is a per-take check, not a constant. **Honest limit:** one bed and one cut point - 1.2x may be luck, and what this establishes is the *method* (measure the wrap) plus the direction; whether another bed is as loop-friendly is an E9-style repeat. **And the expectation was wrong again** - the third time in this scenario that the number and the image corrected me. |

## PENDING

- [ ] **Levels: normalise, or measure how often a take clips.** Three experiments have each found a take peaking at **0.0 dBFS** (the 5 s stinger, both 120 s songs) while others sit at -1.3 to -1.6, so some takes ride the ceiling. Measure the peak distribution over ~10 takes and whether an `AudioAdjustVolume` step before the save fixes it - this is a defect to settle *before* anyone auditions, not a preference.
- [ ] **Duration against lyric length.** E3 sang 344 words in 120 s and the outro never arrived (ASR recall 74%). Sweep a fixed lyric over durations (60/120/180/240 s) and read the recall back: that turns `duration` from a guess into a rule, so a requested song is not silently truncated.
- [ ] **Prompt adherence, measurably (candidate).** Everything above says *is it music* and *are the words right*; nothing says *is it the music that was asked for*. A CLAP-style audio-text similarity model (fetchable the same ephemeral way) would score each take against its tags and turn "does 'sad piano' come back sad" into a number - the one verification gap left that a machine could close.
- [ ] **Tool follow-up: an `audio_check.py` in `scripts` (nothing links to it yet, so it is named, not pathed).** E1-E3 settled the verification method by hand three times - level stats, a spectrogram, and (for words) ASR - each with a wheel fetched for the run: `soundfile`, then `faster-whisper`. One command doing all three, with the dependencies declared, is the audio counterpart of `motion_quality.py`, and it is what makes "we have no ears" a stated limit rather than a hole.
- [ ] **Does the loop recipe hold on other content?** E4 found that an 8 s cut from the middle of an ambient bed joins hard at **1.2x** the median step (effectively seamless) while a crossfade made it **31x** worse - on *one* bed at *one* cut point. Repeat on a rhythmic/percussive bed and on a melodic one, and at a couple of cut points, before the recipe is a rule. This is the audio E9.
- [ ] **The other two routes.** MiniMax Music 3 (`MiniMaxMusic3TextEncode`) and Stable Audio (`ConditioningStableAudio` + `AudioEncoderLoader`) - licence first, then cost. And the SFX question: neither is an *effects* model, so a game's footstep/impact palette may need a fourth answer.
- [ ] **Joint audio+video.** `WanSoundImageToVideo` and `LTXV`'s audio VAE sit in `video/`'s deferred backlog; ACE-Step plus one of those is a talking-head/music-video route that spans two scenarios.
