# Audio Experiments Log

One row per run. Verdicts: `PASS` / `FAIL` / `MEH` / `BLOCKED`.

This is the third scenario born from a modality with the node surface already installed and the weights missing - `video/` was the first, and the method is the same: scope by what the nodes need, read the licence, fetch the weights, then run one flow and **measure** it. `PIPELINES.md` was written for this: audio arrives as new `domain` values on the existing operation families (`generate / scene / audio`), not as a new family, and its `subject` vocabulary is the open question recorded there (`clip`, `cue`).

**We have no ears.** Every verdict here rests on measurable proxies plus a spectrogram - see the traps in `README.md`. That is a real limitation of this scenario, stated up front rather than discovered later.

- [ ] **MiniMax Music 3 - the music model to beat, and its weights are already on the box.** Its template ships as a *subgraph* (a UUID node type), but **every node inside it is present**, so a flat flow can be built straight from the node schemas. The INT8 set is downloaded and visible to the loaders: 2.4 GB DiT (int8) + 8.6 GB pruned-int8 encoder + 0.2 GB DAV VAE = **11.4 GB**, and ComfyUI's own guidance is int8 plus `tiled_decode` for low-VRAM cards. Licence: MiniMax-Music3 Community - a "MiniMax-Music3" attribution in a commercial product's UI, a $20M revenue threshold, and an Acceptable Use Policy.
- [ ] **ACE-Step 1.5 XL - a quality upgrade to the flow we already ship, for the same licence.** `audio_ace_step1_5_xl_{base,sft,turbo}` are *flat* templates, and the XL is a 4B DiT (~9.5 GB bf16) against our 2B turbo, under the same MIT. Worth one measured A/B on S19 - with the card's own caveat that XL wants >=20 GB VRAM without offload, which this 24 GB card meets only with offload.
- [ ] **Stable Audio 3 Medium - newer than the one just shipped.** Its template is a subgraph but all 17 inner nodes are present. Files: `stable_audio_3_medium.safetensors`, plus a Qwen3.5-2B and a t5gemma text encoder (from `Comfy-Org/stable-audio-3` and `Comfy-Org/Qwen3.5`).

**Blocked on a ComfyUI update** - the nodes are simply not in our build; the templates package is ahead of it:
- [ ] **Mel-Band RoFormer - audio separation in-graph.** Missing `MelBandRoFormerModelLoader` and `MelBandRoFormerSampler`. The model is already down (**`MelBandRoformer_fp16.safetensors`, 435 MB, 228M params**) and the **licence is resolved: GPL-3.0** - the checkpoint's author states she added it (the ComfyUI port carries no tag; the implementation lineage - lucidrains' BS-RoFormer and ZFTurbo's training code - is MIT). GPL constrains *distribution* rather than *use*, so stems for a game are fine and only a shipped derivative model would not be; the repo's "never commit weights" rule already avoids that. When unblocked this replaces E10's four-dependency separator chain and makes "a style reference should be a stem" cheap.
- [ ] **Chatterbox TTS - the voice cell.** Missing `FL_ChatterboxTTS`, `FL_ChatterboxMultilingualTTS` and `FL_ChatterboxVC` (plus `AudioCrop`). Speech, voice cloning and multilingual - the one audio cell this scenario has never had.
- [x] **YuE 2's cover/restyle route - NON-COMMERCIAL, but it works. Done (E12-E12d).** It runs here (3.96 GB int8, ~6.2 GB VRAM, a 120 s take in 385 s), and **the cover is the capability ACE-Step cannot reach**: SheetSage2 transcribes the reference's melody into ABC notation and `YuE2GenerateMusic(mode='melody')` re-renders it with a new style and new words - the user's ears confirm the cover is recognisably the reference's tune, restyled, and a 30 s cover costs 65 s. **Its text2music loses to ACE-Step** on identical input (~72 transcribed words against 19, and ACE-Step is MIT), and the template's own defaults come back instrumental. **The weights are `cc-by-nc-4.0`, so every output is research only** - the shippable restyle route is ACE-Step's own Cover mode (MIT, upstream's app), which is the next thing to cost out. Shipped as S22 (`audio/workflows/yue2-cover.json`); the measured arms stay in `workflows/scratch/`, and `ace-step-e8-ballad.json` preserves the ACE-Step side of the comparison.

Worth doing as a batch, not for one model.
