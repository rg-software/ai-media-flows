# Video Experiments Log

One row per run. Verdicts: `PASS` / `FAIL` / `MEH` / `BLOCKED`.

This scenario is **motion as content**: the deliverable is a *video file*, which a viewer plays rather than an engine importing it. That is why it is separate from `animation/`, whose artefacts are engine assets (a sprite sheet, a rig). The two share a border and cross-reference: the video-reference route here is also the answer for animation's "motion that exists only as footage" cell, and `../animation/motion-sourcing.md` is the decision doc for when to reach for it instead of a free clip library.

The rows below were measured while building the Wan Animate 2 flow; E-numbers are a log, not a version, so they are not renumbered (animation/ keeps E4/E6/E7/E8/E15/E16, which measured the SAM 3D Body flow).
